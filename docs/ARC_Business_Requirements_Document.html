2. Executive Summary — What Is ARC?

ARC closes the gap between automated legal paper production and actual money recovery for PaintCo dealer receivables.

QuestionAnswer
What is ARC?A human-gated, multi-day workflow system with eight AI agents (A1–A8) that reconcile receivables, prioritise recovery, recommend notices, orchestrate field visits, guard Section 138 eligibility, draft legal documents, and assemble court-ready case files.
What problem?2,526 notices / ₹193.42 Cr chased in one month — but only 1 dealer visited, 0 rejections, unreconciled AR, manual S138 chain, no statutory clocks.
Two workflowsWorkflow A — Monthly ODOS demand-notice cycle. Workflow B — Section 138 legal pipeline after 60 days non-payment and cheque bounce.
Who uses it?Depot Manager, TSI, Depot Admin, ASM/HO, Legal, Advocate, Finance Controller
What AI doesAnalyses, prioritises, recommends, drafts, orchestrates — via governed tools. Does not approve or compute legal amounts.
What humans doApprove notices (G1), e-sign (G2), approve legal progression (G3), review case file (G4), confirm field PTP, correct cheque data

6. Proposed ARC Solution

ARC addresses nine sub-problems (SP1–SP9) through eight agents, two workflows, four human gates, and a deterministic rules engine — in Shadow, Assisted, or Live mode.

SP1 Ledger reconciliationNet recoverable exposure + line-item audit trailA1

SP3 Recoverability scoringRanked worklist; top-decile in secondsA2
SP4 Notice decisioningIssue / Hold / Reconcile with cited evidenceA3
SP5 S138 eligibilityValidate debt, presentation, return reason before physical chainA4

7. Scope

7.1 In Scope

  • Reconciliation, entity resolution, recoverability scoring, notice decisioning
  • Four HITL gates (Depot Manager, Advocate, Legal ×2)
  • S138 eligibility, limitation clocks, notice drafting, evidence assembly
  • Field visit orchestration, PTP capture, broken-PTP escalation
  • Shadow / Assisted / Live modes; durable multi-day workflows

7.2 Out of Scope

  • Replacement of SAP / core ERP
  • Court filing execution, hearings, arrest warrant automation (portal tracks; no agent defined)
  • Payment collection / banking transactions
  • Autonomous legal action without human gates

23. Summary

ARC in one page:
PaintCo has ~2,900 dealers. When receivables go overdue (>90 days, >₹5,000), ARC runs two workflows: ODOS demand-notice recovery and Section 138 legal escalation (after 60 days non-payment + cheque bounce). Eight AI agents reconcile AR, prioritise dealers, recommend notices, orchestrate field visits, guard legal eligibility, draft documents, and assemble evidence — always through governed tools and human gates. The backend rules engine owns all amounts, dates, and eligibility. Humans approve at four gates. Read Sections 9–19 flowcharts to understand the complete process.
